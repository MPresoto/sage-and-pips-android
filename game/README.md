# Sage & Pips: Snack Quest

A small browser platform adventure hosted alongside the Sage & Pips care app.

## How to play

- Choose Sage for a stronger jump or Pips for faster movement.
- Move with the left/right arrow keys or A/D. On a phone, hold the on-screen arrows.
- Jump with Space, Up, or W; on a phone, use the JUMP button.
- Collect salmon and biscuit treats to restore one energy point. Visit the automatic feeder to recover up to two.
- The cats start with five energy points. A monster collision takes one point. Jump onto a monster to bounce it away.
- Reach the paw gate at the far end of the meadow.
- Use the sound button to mute or turn on the original chiptune loop and effects.

The game uses original generated pixel art and a synthesized soundtrack. It does not use Mario or Nintendo artwork or music.
